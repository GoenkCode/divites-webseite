Privacy policy

This is a translation. The German version is legally binding: Datenschutz.

This site is deliberately built to be frugal. It loads no fonts, maps or scripts from third-party servers, sets no cookies and uses no analytics tools.

Controller

The controller within the meaning of Art. 4(7) GDPR is Marta Danilkovich. Address and contact details are given in the legal notice.

What happens when you visit

The site is delivered via GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When a page is requested, your browser transmits technically necessary data such as your IP address, the time, the file requested and the headers sent. Without this data, no page can be delivered.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest lies in being able to provide the site at all and to protect it against attacks.

Processing takes place on GitHub servers, including in the United States. On 10 July 2023 the European Commission found that companies certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework offer an adequate level of protection. GitHub is certified under it.

GitHub stores access data in server logs. We have no influence on how long or how much is stored; we keep no logs of our own. Details are in GitHub’s privacy statement.

City filter

The filter on the concerts page runs entirely in your browser. No data is transmitted and nothing is stored, neither as a cookie nor anywhere else on your device.

Enquiries

This site has no contact form. You can reach us by email at the address on the contact page; there is no server that receives input and no service provider that processes it.

We process your email in order to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where it concerns steps prior to entering into a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete enquiries once they have been dealt with, unless a statutory retention obligation applies.

Videos

The Music page offers concert videos from YouTube, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

As long as you do not click on a video, no connection to Google is made. No preview image is loaded either. The areas on the page are pure design with no content from Google.

Only when you start a video does your browser connect to YouTube. Your IP address is then transmitted to Google, and Google may store information on your device and collect further data. If you are logged in to Google at the time, the request can be linked to your account.

The legal basis is your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, which you give by clicking on a video. You can withdraw it at any time by reloading the page and not starting another video. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

You can also watch every video directly on YouTube. There is a link below each area for this purpose.

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on a legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR).

To exercise them, contact the address given in the legal notice.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The competent authority is the Hessian Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Der Hessische Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit), PO Box 3163, 65021 Wiesbaden, Germany.
