What the quartet brings

Two violins, a viola, a cello. Every arrangement is the quartet’s own. It plays acoustically, without amplifiers and without electricity. A hall, a church, a foyer: wherever four chairs fit and it is quiet enough, it works.

Between the pieces, there is talking. Short, with humour, no programme-note prose.

Venues so far include the Friedrich-von-Thiersch-Saal at the Kurhaus Wiesbaden, the Literaturhaus Frankfurt, Gießen, Darmstadt and Sulzbach am Main.

Enquiries

Four details are enough for a reply: date, venue, occasion and how long you would like the quartet to play. If the programme isn’t settled yet, that is no obstacle. Suggestions will follow.

A short, informal email to info@danilkovich.com will do.
