The Ensemble

Violin

  • Violinist from Minsk, based in Frankfurt am Main since 2013. Studied at the Belarusian State Academy of Music. As a soloist and chamber musician she has played at Weill Recital Hall at Carnegie Hall and the Mozarteum Salzburg, among others. She founded the Divites Quartett and directs it. Her violin was made by M. Deconet around 1750.

    Marta Danilkovich

    Founder and director

  • Violinist from South Korea, born in 1994. Concert diploma from the Frankfurt University of Music and Performing Arts. Prizewinner at the Lipizer Violin Competition, the Asian International Competition and the Pirastro Pirazzi Competition. In South Korea she recorded soundtracks for K-pop, TV series and films.

    Namhyun Kim

  • Violinist. She had her first lesson at ten and left home at eleven to become a musician. She came to Frankfurt by way of Weimar. She plays in orchestras, chamber ensembles and jazz bands, and picks up any instrument that comes within reach.

    Anna Rothe

  • Violinist from Bulgaria. Solo debut at nine. First prizes at Mozart for Young Musicians and at the Koczian and Dobrin Petkov competitions. Concerts in Europe and the USA, award-winning CD recordings, including a Blue Ribbon Award. She has taught at Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz since 2013. Her Alessandro Mezzadri violin (1720) is on loan from Guarneri Hall NFP and Darnton & Hersh Fine Violins, Chicago.

    Irina Borissova

Viola

  • Violist from South Korea. She gained orchestral and chamber music experience at Theater Baden-Baden, the Festspielhaus Baden-Baden and Theater Osnabrück. In Frankfurt she teaches both violin and viola.

    Eunseon Oh

  • Violist from Kraków, raised in a family of musicians. Studied in Kraków, Łódź and Aachen, now at home in Gießen. Her viola has taken her to China, Brazil, India, Israel, Mexico, the USA and many other countries.

    Karolina Rybka

Cello

  • Cellist, composer and arranger. ECHO Klassik 2011. Studied in Karlsruhe and Lucerne, more than fifty works of his own. Over a hundred concerts a year in Europe, the USA and Asia, at the Elbphilharmonie, the Barbican Centre in London and everywhere in between.

    Victor aus Butzbach

  • Cellist from Freiburg im Breisgau. Studied with Adriana Contino at the Freiburg University of Music. A member of the Philharmonic Orchestra Gießen since 1999 and its principal cellist since 2014. Concert tours to France, Mexico, China, Russia and Brazil. Since 2022 a member of the Bayreuth Festival Orchestra.

    Attila Hündöl

  • Cellist. She performs as a soloist and in ensembles of every size, and develops new concert formats that bring art forms together. Her repertoire runs from the Baroque through jazz to contemporary music.

    Larissa Nagel
