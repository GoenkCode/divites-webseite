About the ensemble

Divites comes from the Latin dives: rich. Here it stands for a rich repertoire across every kind of genre, from the classics through film music and disco hits to modern rock and heavy metal.

Vivaldi. Metallica. Hans Zimmer. Queen. Four string instruments, original arrangements, not a single amplifier. What sounds like a stadium in the original sounds like a concert hall here. And still works.

Between the pieces, there is talking. Short, with humour, no programme-note prose. You find out why a piece made the programme, and laugh along the way. Afraid of musicology? No need.

Marta Danilkovich founded the quartet in 2020. It plays by candlelight, at the Jahrhunderthalle in Frankfurt, the Kurhaus in Wiesbaden, the Schlosstheater in the Neues Palais in Berlin. Most concerts sell out.

The quartet is a circle of musicians who take turns, each with a career of their own: the principal cellist of a state theatre, a prizewinner of the Mendelssohn Competition and an ECHO Klassik winner.
